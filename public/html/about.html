About Green Margin Capital

A Kenyan organic investment house with a solid reputation and deep experience of the continent's capital markets

Founded in 2009
Our Story

Building Trust Through Expertise

Green Margin Capital was founded on the principle of democratizing access to investment solutions. With over 15 years of experience navigating the complexities of East African capital markets, we've built a reputation for integrity, expertise, and client-focused service.

Our team combines deep local knowledge with international best practices, providing our clients with sophisticated investment strategies that are grounded in the realities of the African market. We understand the opportunities and challenges unique to this dynamic region, and we leverage this insight to deliver superior returns for our clients.

Our Mission

To empower individuals and institutions across Africa with accessible, sophisticated investment solutions that drive wealth creation and financial independence.

Our Vision

To be East Africa's most trusted investment partner, recognized for innovation, integrity, and exceptional client outcomes in capital markets.
