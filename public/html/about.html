About Green Margin Capital

A dynamic, Kenya-based capital markets and stockbroker democratising access to investment solutions across Africa

Founded in 2023
Who We Are

An Organic Kenyan Investment House

Green Margin Capital is positioned as a dynamic, Kenya-based capital markets and stockbroker operating from The Exchange building in Westlands. We bring a blend of established market expertise, regional insight, and ambition to scale across the continent.

Founded with a clear purpose—to use cutting-edge technology to democratize access to investment opportunities—we're making financial markets more inclusive, transparent, and beneficial to a broader spectrum of Kenyan investors.

As a fully licensed intermediary (CMA Licence #253), we operate with deep understanding of regulatory regimes, including the Capital Markets Authority, Central Depository & Settlement Corporation, and the Nairobi Securities Exchange.

Our Vision

To promote financial empowerment by delivering strategic capital markets investment solutions that are ethical, research-driven, and accessible—becoming the leading African investment house that bridges local opportunity and global capital.

Our Mission

To empower investors through transparent, reliable, and client-focused services grounded in strong governance and innovation—supporting enterprise growth, financial inclusion, and market development across Africa.
