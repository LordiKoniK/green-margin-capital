What is a CDSC Account?

The Central Depository and Settlement Corporation (CDSC) is Kenya's securities depository that holds shares electronically on behalf of investors.

Secure Storage

Your shares are held electronically in a secure, regulated environment, eliminating risks of loss, theft, or forgery

Real-Time Trading

Execute trades instantly on the Nairobi Securities Exchange with immediate settlement and confirmation

24/7 Access

Monitor your portfolio anytime, anywhere through the online CDSC portal and receive instant notifications

Easy Transfers

Transfer shares between accounts seamlessly without physical paperwork or lengthy processing times

Dividend Management

Receive dividends directly to your bank account with automated processing and detailed statements

Detailed Records

Get comprehensive statements showing all your holdings, transactions, and corporate actions in one place
